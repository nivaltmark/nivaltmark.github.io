The other half of the product

What a client is given

their own login · their own space · the same live data, scoped to them

A client does not get a report emailed to them on a Friday. They get an account, and behind it a space that holds only their own work: their campaigns and what each one is actually doing, their pipeline, the replies that came back, and the documents the engagement runs on: the onboarding answers, the discovery notes, the ideal-customer profiles, the approved copy. The same tables the agency reads, scoped to one client and nothing else.

The staff view can step into any client’s space to see exactly what they see, and the screen says so while it is doing it. The second agency on the platform runs it under its own brand, with its own clients inside it, which is the reason the tenancy is real rather than a column called client_id.

Client work · three businesses

Systems for outside clients

n8n · WooCommerce · Supabase + pgvector · Google Places, Routes and Maps · WhatsApp

Three businesses outside software, each with one piece no vendor solved. They have nothing else in common, so each is its own case below.

an online furniture store

A shop that keeps its own assistant honest

WooCommerce · Supabase Postgres + pgvector · OpenAI embeddings · a sales agent

  • ProblemA shop assistant that quotes a price from an old document is worse than none, and the catalogue and the current discount change all the time.
  • BuiltThe catalogue is rebuilt from the live store every night and patched the moment a product changes; the current discount lives in one row, and the assistant reads both before it answers the customer.
  • ResultAn assistant that answers from the live catalogue and the current offer, and a suite that also restyles every product photo and runs the shop’s social feed.

a land brokerage in the United States

A listing kit that draws its own property line

state cadastral ArcGIS services · Mapbox + Google Static Maps · Places + Routes · Claude · GPT · Gemini

  • ProblemA land listing needs the exact parcel with its legal boundary drawn, and the United States has no national parcel-geometry API.
  • BuiltOne form of eighteen fields in; three sales texts, satellite renders with the boundary drawn, and nearby places with real driving times out. It asks each state’s own cadastral service, and resolves the county first where a state publishes nothing.
  • ResultThe parcel problem was solved without a single AI model in the mapping engine. The brokerage never went live with it; it is here because that part is worth being asked about.

a payroll bureau

A clerk drops a spreadsheet, WhatsApp answers with the report

an n8n form · Excel parsing · a Hebrew right-to-left PDF · WhatsApp delivery

  • ProblemThe bureau wanted its monthly payroll checked and reported, and did not want software: no dashboard, no login.
  • BuiltA clerk drops the payroll export on a web page. Six checks written out of labour law run on it, and the finished Hebrew report comes back on WhatsApp as a PDF.
  • ResultOnly exceptions are reported, so a clean month is one green line. Two populations, on 182- and 154.8-hour months, run on the same code.
